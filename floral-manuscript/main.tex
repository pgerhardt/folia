\cvsection{Profile}
Illustrator and designer translating botanical research into clear, expressive
publications. Six years of experience across field guides, museum interpretation,
and editorial systems. Combines close observation, patient craft, and practical
production skills to make complex subjects inviting.

\cvsection{Experience}
\role{Senior Editorial Designer}{2023--Present}{Fieldnote Studio\enspace /\enspace Denver, CO}
\begin{achievements}
 \item Lead visual development for illustrated books, exhibition guides, and
 seasonal publications, from early sketches to press-ready files.
 \item Built a modular page system for a 240-page regional flora, coordinating
 180 illustrations with three contributing botanists.
 \item Standardized proofing and preflight checklists across a six-person team,
 reducing average correction rounds from four to two.
\end{achievements}
\role{Illustrator \& Graphic Designer}{2020--2023}{Juniper Natural History Museum\enspace /\enspace Boulder, CO}
\begin{achievements}
 \item Created specimen illustrations, interpretive panels, and visitor maps
 for exhibitions on native plants and pollinator habitats.
 \item Designed an accessible family field journal used in 24 school workshops,
 with clear visual prompts and space for observation.
 \item Prepared color-managed artwork and coordinated production with local
 printers, fabricators, and the museum education team.
\end{achievements}

\role{Flora of the Front Range}{2025}{Illustrated field guide\enspace /\enspace Fieldnote Studio}
Designed a portable identification guide with a consistent specimen scale,
habitat icons, and a visual index. Developed botanical plates from field sketches
and refined diagnostic details with scientific reviewers.
\role{The Pollinator Garden}{2024}{Exhibition identity\enspace /\enspace Juniper Museum}
Created an illustrated identity and a suite of 18 interpretation panels.
Balanced botanical detail with large-format readability and supplied reusable
templates for future seasonal updates.
\role{A Cabinet of Seeds}{2022}{Independent artist's book\enspace /\enspace Edition of 100}
Produced a hand-bound collection pairing fine-line seed drawings with natural-history
notes. Managed concept, typography, paper selection, printing, and binding.
